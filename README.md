**Vice Cover Studio v2** — turn your photos and a caption into a GTA-style multi-panel poster, and export it as a PNG. Ten layouts (Hero + 3, Grid 4, Grid 9, Full bleed, Tilted snap, Slashed key art, Whole-cast 6, Comic strip, Diagonal duo, Poster triptych), seven colour grades (Sunset, Neon Night, Vice City, Heat Wave, Poster Ink, Golden Hour, Palm Noir), undo/redo (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y), a Surprise me randomiser, and a per-panel photo engine — drag, zoom, flip and rotate each panel, with face-aware smart fill. Bold Anton lettering with a neon Pacifico subtitle, a heat-star meter and a badge ribbon, in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 and 1920×1080. Everything runs offline in the browser: photos are read on your own machine and never uploaded.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.mjs
```

Then open http://localhost:8123 — pick photos, click a panel and a photo to fill it, drag a photo to move it, and hit **Export PNG**. `?shot=editor` and `?shot=cover` set up fixed scenes for screenshots (layout, grade, size, caption, subtitle, badge, heat and photos are query parameters).
